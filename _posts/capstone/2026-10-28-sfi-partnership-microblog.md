    <div class="partner-kicker">01 / Community partner</div>
    <h2 id="partner-about">Who we serve</h2>
    <p class="partner-lead"><strong>SFI Foundation, Inc.</strong> is a nonprofit founded in 1978 that develops and administers standards, certification programs, and testing criteria for specialty performance and racing safety equipment. Its community includes <strong>manufacturers, racing sanctioning bodies, racers, and teams</strong> who need reliable motorsports safety information.</p>
