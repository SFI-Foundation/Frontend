  <div class="sfi-sect-label">Who we serve</div>
  <h2 class="sfi-sect-title">Building for the motorsports safety community</h2>
  <p class="sfi-sect-desc">SFI Foundation, Inc. is a nonprofit founded in 1978. It develops and administers motorsports equipment specifications and quality-assurance programs for manufacturers, racing sanctioning bodies, racers, and teams. Our student team is continuing the prior semester's website prototype, exploring ways to help people find and understand safety information. Specific features and a production rollout require SFI's feedback and approval.</p>
