## Who we serve — SFI Foundation

**SFI Foundation, Inc.** is a nonprofit serving the motorsports and specialty performance equipment community. Founded in 1978, it develops and administers standards, certification programs, and testing criteria for racing safety equipment. Its work connects **equipment manufacturers, racing sanctioning bodies, racers, and teams** who rely on clear safety specifications.

- **Official organization website:** [www.sfifoundation.com](https://www.sfifoundation.com/)
- **What the organization does:** [About SFI](https://www.sfifoundation.com/about-sfi-foundation/)
- **How to contact the organization:** [Official contact](https://www.sfifoundation.com/contact/)
- **Student website project:** [SFI Foundation frontend](https://github.com/SFI-Foundation/Frontend) · [backend](https://github.com/SFI-Foundation/Backend)

### Our capstone partnership and website

The Del Norte High School Computer Science team is continuing an SFI website prototype from the previous semester. We aim to help people discover, understand, and organize motorsports safety information more easily, and to explore improvements to SFI's online presence. Current student-prototype areas include searchable specifications, assisted equipment discovery, and gear-tracking workflows. Specific priorities, scope, production readiness, and acceptance **must be confirmed with SFI**; SFI's interest in collaboration does not imply that individual features have been approved or deployed.

The site in this repository is a **student development project**. Always refer to SFI's official website and documentation for authoritative standards and certification information.
