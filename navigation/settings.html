Language

The application interface is available in English and Spanish. Other languages are partial previews with English fallback. Official specifications, documents and user content are not translated.

Notifications

Communication availability

Specification updates, revision alerts, manufacturer announcements, reminders and optional email delivery are not available in this website. No subscription is created here.

Essential account security communications are separate from optional updates. This page does not change your authentication or security protections.

Privacy & Data

Your local data

Guest preferences stay on this browser. Signed-in presentation preferences are stored with your account. Authentication, saved gear and onboarding answers are separate and are never cleared here.

This website uses authentication cookies for sign-in. Settings does not add analytics or advertising cookies. Account data export and deletion are not available here. Contact SFI for privacy requests and current policy information.
